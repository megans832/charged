cashback credit terms

how your earnings work — the important stuff, in plain english

the big one — non-refundable credit

all cashback you earn through charged is applied to your energy account as non-refundable credit. this means it's real money working directly against your energy costs — but it cannot be cashed out, transferred, or paid to a bank account. it lives on your energy account and that's where it does its job.

think of it like a voucher that's already been added to your account, rather than cash in your pocket. the upside? it gets there automatically, with no action from you.

what non-refundable credit means for you

  • your credit reduces the amount you owe on your energy account
  • you can choose to apply it to your next payment or spread it across upcoming bills
  • it cannot be withdrawn as cash
  • it cannot be transferred to another account or person
  • unused credit does not expire, but remains tied to your energy account
  • if you switch supplier, credits already applied before notice of switch are not transferable

how credit gets to your account

charged collects cashback earned from partner retailers and distributes it in batches to your energy account. credits are typically processed within 30 days of a qualifying transaction being confirmed. travel bookings may take longer, as cashback is paid once your stay or trip is completed.

you'll receive a whatsapp notification when credit lands on your account.

your energy account & direct debit

charged is not an energy supplier. your energy account, direct debit, and billing remain entirely with your supplier. charged simply adds credit to your account — we do not handle, change, or access your direct debit or billing in any way.

earning cashback — how it works

  • card-linked offers: link your payment card in the charged app. when you spend at a partner brand, cashback is tracked automatically — no codes or clicking needed
  • affiliate links via whatsapp: message charged to request a cashback link before you buy. click the link, complete your purchase, and cashback is tracked to your account
  • cashback rates are set by the retailer and may change. charged does not guarantee rates
  • qualifying purchases only — gift cards and purchases made with store credit may not qualify

spreading your credit

once credit is on your energy account, you may be able to apply it to your next payment or spread it across upcoming payments, depending on your supplier's options. charged will guide you via whatsapp.

the small print

charged reserves the right to amend these terms. you'll be notified of any material changes via whatsapp. continued use after notification constitutes acceptance. questions? message us on whatsapp anytime.
