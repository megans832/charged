frequently
asked questions

everything you need to know about signing up, linking your card and getting your energy bill credit.

Q. how do i sign up?
press here and we'll open up whatsapp with a message ready to send — just hit send and we'll take it from there.
Q. how do i link my card?
follow all of the onboarding steps, then type link card in the chat.
Q. how do i see which brands have offers?
just type which brands are on offer in the whatsapp chat and we'll send you the live list.
Q. can i type an individual brand to see if it's there?
yup, just type something like what you got for nike and charged will help you out.
Q. how do i unlink my card?
type remove card in the chat at any time and follow the steps.
Q. what if the bot hallucinates?
ha! it is always possible that bots can hallucinate. just send a note to help@getcharged.energy and we'll be in touch.
Q. where do i hear about company or brand updates?
follow us on tiktok @getcharged.energy and instagram @getcharged.energyuk for daily updates on products, brands and system changes.
Q. how do i redeem my credit?
credit will automatically be sent to your energy bill once it reaches the threshold of £10 — nothing for you to do.
Q. can i chat with the bot?
my my! yes of course you can — chat to our bot if you're lonely 💛
Q. can i refer a friend?
yes — you can refer up to 5 friends. you'll receive £5 energy bill credit when they make their first purchase.
