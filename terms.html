The Energy Vault & Applying Credit to Your Energy Account

Energy Bill Credit which has accrued to your Account, but which has not yet been applied to your Energy Account, is held as a record in your Energy Vault. As set out in "Our Responsibility for Loss or Damage" above, Energy Bill Credit in your Energy Vault is not your property, has no cash value, is not held on trust or in any segregated account for you, and does not represent a claim against any specific fund or asset.

We periodically apply accrued Energy Bill Credit to your Energy Account (currently on a monthly cycle) by making a payment to your Energy Provider, as a commercial creditor of that Energy Provider, using the services of our Payment Service Provider(s). Your Energy Provider then applies the corresponding value as a credit to your Energy Account, under separate commercial arrangements between us and your Energy Provider.

We are not your payment service provider. We do not execute payment transactions on your instruction or on your behalf, and we do not, at any time, hold funds belonging to you. Our Payment Service Provider(s) act on our instruction, using our own funds, to discharge our own commercial obligations to your Energy Provider — they do not provide payment services to you, and you have no direct relationship with them in connection with the Service.

When Energy Bill Credit can be applied

Energy Bill Credit is typically subject to a pending period before it becomes eligible for application to your Energy Account, to allow for returns, disputes, or transaction reversals at the relevant Merchant. This period varies by Merchant and offer and will be indicated in the Service where relevant.

If we do not receive the expected value from a Merchant, if you return a purchase, or if a transaction is invalidated, disputed, or otherwise does not settle, some or all of the associated pending Energy Bill Credit will be removed from your Energy Vault, at our sole discretion.

Energy Bill Credit may change or be revoked

We may modify the way Energy Bill Credit is calculated or applied at any time, for any reason, at our sole discretion. Once applied to your Energy Account, an application of Energy Bill Credit is final and cannot be reversed, exchanged, or refunded by us — however, we reserve the right to recover the value of any Energy Bill Credit we later determine was applied in error, fraudulently, or in breach of these Terms, whether by adjusting future accruals or by other lawful means.
